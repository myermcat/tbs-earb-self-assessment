Resolved

Settled, and how.

Should the tool hold classified information?
Resolved. No. Nothing protected or classified goes in at all, settled with Dan on 2 September. That removes the second deployment, the local-only engine, the theme switch, and the question of recalling something that should never have been sent.
What happens to online data when a marking rises?
Resolved. Mostly moot now. Evidence is a link and the tool holds nothing classified. Where a submitted record turns out to be wrong, it is withdrawn and excluded from the statistics, never deleted.
Does a two-topic question count twice overall?
Resolved. No. Once in the overall score, and at full weight inside each topic's own roll-up, which is where the weights genuinely differ.
File sharing for a team filling one in together?
Resolved. No files. Everything is online, so version control stops being a problem the tool has to solve.
Where the plan lives
Resolved. One page you can read in a browser, this one. The Markdown version is gone.
Deletion rights
Resolved. Nothing is ever hard-deleted. A record is withdrawn and left out of the statistics.
One build or two?
Resolved. One. Submission is a capability the hosting turns on.
